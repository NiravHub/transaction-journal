About Transaction Journal

Transaction Journal helps you keep track of your daily transactions in one place.

Instead of searching through screenshots, bank statements, or payment apps, you can save:

  • Transaction amount
  • Date & time
  • Payment method
  • Location
  • Notes
  • Receipt screenshots

The goal is simple: make it easy to remember where, when, and why a payment was made.

Features

  • Save transaction details
  • Upload payment screenshots
  • Search transactions
  • Track payment methods
  • Access records from any device
  • Secure user accounts

Why I Built This

Many payments are forgotten after a few days. I wanted a simple way to store transaction information along with notes and receipts so everything stays organized.

How to Add a Transaction

  1. Click on + Add Transaction
  2. Enter a transaction title & amount.
  3. Select the date and time.
  4. Choose the payment method.
  5. Enter the location (optional) & Add notes (optional).
  6. Upload a receipt or payment screenshot (optional).
  7. Click Save Transaction.
  8. Your transaction will appear in the dashboard and can be searched later.
